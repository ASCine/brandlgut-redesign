Farm & wine

Our farm

The Brandlgut farm was built in the late 19th century and we took it over in 2006. After several years of renovation we have been welcoming guests all year round since July 2016.

Wine & garden

Our Lagrein grows on two hectares in a prime location. A smaller part of the farm is reserved for fruit and vegetables – apples, pears, cherries, cucumbers, pumpkins – for our guests and for ourselves.

Would you like to learn more about our work as winemakers? We are happy to show you around – just ask us.

Breakfast

In the breakfast room, built entirely of spruce wood, the day starts with coffee, fresh rolls, local delicacies and eggs from our own hens. It is where we most enjoy talking to our guests.

Terrace & lawn

The large shared terrace and the sunbathing lawn offer a wide view over the valley. Children have plenty of room to play, a play corner with a sandbox and a big swing – and anyone who likes playing with dogs will love Mira.
