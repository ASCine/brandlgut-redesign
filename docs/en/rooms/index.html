Good to know

  • Prices & breakfast

    Prices are per room and night, for three nights or more. Breakfast is served daily from 8:00 to 10:00 a.m. for €18 per person.

  • Arrival & departure

    Check-in 5:00 – 8:00 p.m., check-out 9:00 – 10:30 a.m. Unannounced check-in after 8:00 p.m. costs €20 per hour. Arrival is not possible after 10:00 p.m.

  • Children

    Free up to the age of three, €15 per night from four to six. At breakfast, children from four to nine pay €4 less and toddlers nothing.

  • Parking

    A parking space is reserved for you. Please tell us in advance about larger vehicles; a trailer costs €3 per day.

  • Non-smoking house

    There is no smoking in the house or the rooms – you are welcome to smoke on your balcony or the terrace.

  • Pets

    Unfortunately we cannot accommodate pets: free-range chickens, dogs, cats and other small animals live on the farm.

  • Cancellation

    For cancellations fewer than 14 days before arrival, no-shows or early departures we charge 100 % of the total price.

  • Cleaning & towels

    On Sundays we clean the rooms on request only. Towels are changed every three days, more often for a surcharge.
