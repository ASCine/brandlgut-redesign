Good to know

  • Prices & breakfast

    Our prices are quoted per night and room at a minimum stay of three nights. The breakfast is not included. For 18 € per day and person you can enjoy our daily breakfast from 8:00 to 10:00 a.m.

  • Arrival & departure

    We are looking forward upon your arrival between 5:00 p.m. and 8:00 p.m. Please note that we charge additional 20 € per hour for a check-in after 8:00 p.m. without prior notification or agreement, past 10:00 p.m. an arrival is not possible. Our check-out time is 9:00 to 10:30 a.m.

  • Children

    Kids: Up to the age of three they are free of charge; the four, five and six year old relish their stay for only 15 € per night. All kids which are four to nine years old get a discount of 4 € per day at our breakfast.

  • Parking

    Car parking is a matter of course. You have a bigger vehicle than a usual car? Please let us know in advance so we can keep an appropriate parking space. A trailer is charged with an extra fee of 3 € per day.

  • Non-smoking house

    Inside our house as well as inside all closed rooms smoking is completely forbidden. Please use the balcony or one of our terraces and simultaneously take advantage of the great view and the fresh air South Tyrol has to provide.

  • Pets

    Unfortunately, all your pets have to stay at home – we are keeping free-range chicken at our farm and we own dogs, cats and other small animals which are not used to foreign animals.

  • Cancellation

    In the event of a short-notice cancellation fewer than 14 days and non-arrival or early departure, a minimum of 100 % of the cost of the (remaining) holiday will be invoiced.

  • Cleaning & towels

    Room cleaning: On Sundays, rooms are only cleaned upon request. Towels are changed every 3 days. For extra changes we apply a charge.
